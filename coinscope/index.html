Know before you trade.

CoinScope is a bookmark, not an extension. Save it once — click it on any token page in Axiom or Padre, and a full Solana risk scan opens in a new tab, loaded automatically for the coin you're viewing. Every scan checks for rug pulls, insider wallet networks, and the coordinated wallets that drain traders before they even notice.

Built by traders, for traders CoinScope is a community tool made by a small group of devs who used to trade full-time — and got tired of watching wallets get drained by insiders and rug pullers. So they built the scanner they wished they'd had, and now anyone can use it for free before they ape in.

01

Show your bookmarks bar

Press Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows or Cmd+Shift+B on Mac to reveal it.

02

Drag the button into it

Drag the white COINSCOPE button below straight onto your bookmarks bar. One bookmark works everywhere.

03

Open a token, click it

A new tab opens with the full scan — including insider network and rug analysis — already loaded. Close it when you're done, click again for the next coin.
